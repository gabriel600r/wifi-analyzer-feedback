# Privacy Policy — EgeaINC WiFi Analyzer

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

## Introduction

EgeaINC WiFi Analyzer ("the App", listed on Google Play as "Analizador WiFi + Tools" / "WiFi Analyzer: Network Scanner") is developed by EgeaINC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

## Your Measurements Stay on Your Device

**EgeaINC does not collect, store, or receive your measurements.** The App has no account and no EgeaINC server behind it.

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- **WiFi history:** network name (SSID), signal, speeds, pings, public IP and ISP of the analyses you run.
- **Tool histories:** network scans (device IPs, MAC addresses, vendors, names and aliases you assign), port scans, speed tests, traceroutes, Whois lookups, public IP changes, cell measurements and fiber loss calculations.
- **Preferences:** language, favorites, limits and other settings.

This data never leaves your device unless you share or export it yourself, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear it from Settings.

## Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Users with Pro do not see ads, and the App does not start the ads service for them.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:
- The device's advertising ID
- Approximate location derived from the IP address
- Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
- Ad interactions (impressions and taps)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by EgeaINC. Your measurements and histories are never shared with AdMob.

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Your choices:**
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
- Buying Pro (one-time purchase) removes all ads.

## Network Tools and Third-Party Services

Several tools only work by contacting a service on the internet. The App contacts them only when you use that tool, and sends only what the tool needs:

| Tool | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Speed Test | Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com) | Test traffic only |
| Public IP | api.ipify.org, api.my-ip.io, api64.ipify.org | A request to learn your public IP |
| Public IP (network and location) | ipapi.co, ip-api.com, ipwho.is | Your public IP |
| Traceroute (map) | ip-api.com | The IP addresses of the hops |
| Traceroute (map tiles) | CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com) | Map tile requests |
| DNS Lookup | Google (dns.google), Cloudflare, AdGuard or NextDNS, as you choose | The domain you look up |
| Whois | rdap.org and the registry it points to | The domain you look up |
| Breach check (passwords) | Have I Been Pwned (api.pwnedpasswords.com) | Only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 hash, never the password |
| Ping Monitor, home analysis | Your router, 8.8.8.8, 1.1.1.1 or a target you choose | Ping packets |

Network scans, port scans, UPnP discovery, Wake on LAN, cell information, channel analysis, calculators and the fiber color code run on your device and your local network.

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment information.

## Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:
- **Location (precise or approximate):** Android requires it for any app to see nearby WiFi networks and cell information. The App does not use or store your location, and does not send it anywhere.
- **Phone state:** Used only to read the mobile network and cell data shown in Cell Info.
- **WiFi and network state, change WiFi state, multicast:** Used to read your connection, scan networks and discover devices on your local network.
- **Internet:** Required for the network tools listed above, in-app purchases and loading ads in the free version.
- **Wake lock:** Keeps the screen on during long measurements, when you choose to.
- **Advertising ID:** Used by Google AdMob to show ads in the free version.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data. The App includes "Share" and "Export" features that let you manually send reports and images through your device's share sheet (WhatsApp, email, etc.). This is entirely user-initiated and we have no access to the shared content.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.
